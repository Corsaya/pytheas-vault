# Prompt

> lets clear all inconsistencies in the vaults, then I'd like you to make an atlas of every single folder and file in each of the vaults. That finished goal will be after we find approapriate folder organization within the vaults.

# Interpretation

The work is staged: audit and reconcile inconsistencies; capture a complete
pre-organization atlas; classify the collection and propose durable folder
organization; obtain Donovan's judgment on structural choices; then reorganize
and regenerate the atlas as the finished state.
