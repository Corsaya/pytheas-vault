# Prompt

what about the motorola phone with graphene os, is there planned graphene support for pixel 11, better than ios 16 pro? GrapheneOS big because open-source and not completely dominated by Google or Apple (trying to avoid)

# Result

There is no current Motorola with official GrapheneOS support. Motorola and GrapheneOS have a real partnership, but supported flagship devices are expected in 2027; the initial phones are expected to cost more than comparable Pixels. Expected first targets are a conventional flagship, followed by future Razr Fold and Razr Ultra models. Current Motorola phones do not meet GrapheneOS requirements.

Pixel 11 is not a GrapheneOS recommendation today. The project developed a partial port but reported that the phone lacks hardware memory tagging, a cornerstone of GrapheneOS hardening. The project may skip the series; no production support or delivery date is promised. Buy Pixel 10 if buying now, or wait for the 2027 Motorola generation if the point is to reduce dependence on Google hardware.

Against an iPhone 16 Pro, Pixel 10 + GrapheneOS is decisively better for autonomy, privacy controls, Android development, and avoiding platform domination. iPhone remains the better choice only for native iOS development (which requires iOS hardware and a Mac), Apple ecosystem integration, and possibly its particular camera/video workflow. GrapheneOS does not make a Pixel fully Google-independent: the physical hardware, firmware, radios, and some device components remain Google/vendor supplied and proprietary. It does make the operating system open source and removes Google services by default; optional Google Play runs as ordinary sandboxed apps.

Sources consulted: GrapheneOS official FAQ/features; current reporting on the Motorola partnership and 2027 roadmap; current reporting on Pixel 11 MTE and GrapheneOS port status; Google Pixel update documentation.
