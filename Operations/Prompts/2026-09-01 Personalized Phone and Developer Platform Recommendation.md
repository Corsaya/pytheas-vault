# Prompt

what would you recommend for me, knowing all my preferences, if some arent known, ask.

Follow-up context: current device is a working iPhone 16 Pro; there is no deadline or stated budget; intended projects include cross-device life-organization software (Chiron), sports statistics, macro tracking, and an AI-tutor classroom; user wants to grow from AI-assisted prototyping into engineering. Banking, tap-to-pay, fitness tracking (likely Garmin), school, and work apps must function.

# Result

Do not replace the working iPhone 16 Pro now. Retain it as the iOS test device and daily driver while building. Do not buy Pixel 10 merely to obtain GrapheneOS: it would solve the privacy desire today but keeps the hardware tied to Google and introduces a known Google Wallet NFC-payment failure.

Target the 2027 Motorola/GrapheneOS flagships as the potential daily-driver replacement, not a preorder. Buy only after official stable GrapheneOS support, a verified long update commitment, and direct testing of the actual bank, school, work, and Garmin workflows. Garmin Connect is reported to work on GrapheneOS; Garmin Pay could meet the contactless-payment requirement but must be verified against the user's bank/card. Pixel 11 is excluded until GrapheneOS formally resolves the missing hardware memory-tagging issue and ships production support.

Keep the iPhone even after an Android move for iOS testing. A Chiron-like product should be web-first/local-first so it works across desktop, Android, and iOS; the phone choice should be a test target, not the architecture. Before native iOS delivery, determine whether access to a Mac/Xcode is available.

Two required remaining checks: bank/card support for Garmin Pay, and Mac access for iOS development/testing.

Sources consulted: GrapheneOS usage guide and forum discussions on banking, Garmin Connect/Pay, and NFC payments; Apple Developer physical-device testing documentation; current Motorola and Pixel 11 GrapheneOS reporting.
