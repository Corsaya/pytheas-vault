## Questions

**Q1.** (MC) If $5(x + 3) = 4x + 21$, what is the value of $x$?

(A) 2  (B) 4  (C) 6  (D) 12

---

**Q2.** (SPR) A line in the $xy$-plane passes through the point $(0, 9)$ and has a slope of $-\frac{3}{2}$. At what value of $x$ does this line cross the $x$-axis?

---

**Q3.** (MC) What is the slope of the line that passes through the points $(-3, 4)$ and $(5, -12)$?

(A) $-2$  (B) $-\frac{1}{2}$  (C) $\frac{1}{2}$  (D) $2$

---

**Q4.** (SPR)
$$\begin{aligned} 2x + y &= 11 \\ x - y &= 1 \end{aligned}$$
If $(x, y)$ is the solution to the system of equations above, what is the value of $y$?

---

**Q5.** (MC) Line $m$ is parallel to the line $3x + 4y = 12$ and passes through the point $(8, 1)$. What is the $y$-intercept of line $m$?

(A) $-5$  (B) $1$  (C) $7$  (D) $9$

---

**Q6.** (MC) Which of the following equations defines a line perpendicular to the line $y = \frac{1}{5}x - 4$?

(A) $y = \frac{1}{5}x + 2$
(B) $y = -\frac{1}{5}x + 2$
(C) $y = 5x + 2$
(D) $y = -5x + 2$

---

**Q7.** (SPR)
$$\begin{aligned} 3x + ky &= 12 \\ 9x + 15y &= 18 \end{aligned}$$
In the system above, $k$ is a constant. If the system has no solution, what is the value of $k$?

---

**Q8.** (MC) A plumber charges a flat service fee plus an hourly rate. The total charge $C$, in dollars, for a job lasting $h$ hours is given by $C = 45h + 90$. Which of the following is the best interpretation of the number 90 in this equation?

(A) The plumber's hourly rate, in dollars
(B) The flat service fee, in dollars, charged regardless of job length
(C) The total charge, in dollars, for a 2-hour job
(D) The number of hours in the plumber's work week

---

**Q9.** (SPR) The height of a candle, in millimeters, after burning for $t$ hours is modeled by $h = 62 - 4t$. After how many hours is the candle 26 millimeters tall?

---

**Q10.** (MC) A student bought a total of 20 notebooks and pens. Each notebook cost \$3.00 and each pen cost \$1.75. The student spent \$50.00 in total, before tax. How many notebooks did the student buy?

(A) 8  (B) 10  (C) 12  (D) 14

# Answer Key

## Q1 — Answer: C (6)

$$5(x+3) = 4x + 21 \;\Rightarrow\; 5x + 15 = 4x + 21 \;\Rightarrow\; x = 6$$

Check: $5(9) = 45$ and $4(6)+21 = 45$ ✓

> **Trap:** the distractors here come from the two standard slips — distributing the 5 to only the $x$ (giving $5x + 3 = 4x + 21$), or adding $4x$ instead of subtracting it when collecting terms. Distribute to **both** terms inside the parentheses, and move variable terms in one direction only.

---

## Q2 — Answer: 6

The point $(0, 9)$ is the $y$-intercept, so $y = -\frac{3}{2}x + 9$. The $x$-intercept is where $y = 0$:

$$0 = -\tfrac{3}{2}x + 9 \;\Rightarrow\; \tfrac{3}{2}x = 9 \;\Rightarrow\; x = 6$$

> **Trap:** answering 9 — that's the $y$-intercept, not the $x$-intercept. "Crosses the $x$-axis" always means set $y = 0$.

---

## Q3 — Answer: A ($-2$)

$$m = \frac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1} = \frac{-12 - 4}{5 - (-3)} = \frac{-16}{8} = -2$$

> **Trap:** (C) and (D) come from mishandling the double negative in the denominator ($5 - (-3) = 8$, not 2) or from inverting rise and run. Write the subtraction out rather than doing it in your head.

---

## Q4 — Answer: 3

Add the two equations to eliminate $y$:
$$(2x + y) + (x - y) = 11 + 1 \;\Rightarrow\; 3x = 12 \;\Rightarrow\; x = 4$$
Back-substitute: $4 - y = 1 \Rightarrow y = 3$.

Check in the first equation: $2(4) + 3 = 11$ ✓

> **Trap:** the question asks for $y$, but $x$ is what you find first. On a grid-in there is no answer choice to warn you. Circle the requested variable before you start.

---

## Q5 — Answer: C (7)

$3x + 4y = 12 \Rightarrow y = -\frac{3}{4}x + 3$, so the slope is $-\frac{3}{4}$. Parallel lines share that slope.

Point-slope through $(8, 1)$:
$$y - 1 = -\tfrac34 (x - 8) \;\Rightarrow\; y - 1 = -\tfrac34 x + 6 \;\Rightarrow\; y = -\tfrac34 x + 7$$

$y$-intercept $= 7$. Check: at $x = 8$, $y = -6 + 7 = 1$ ✓

> **Trap:** (D) 9 comes from using the perpendicular slope $\frac43$ by reflex. Read whether the question says parallel or perpendicular — they look nearly identical on screen.

---

## Q6 — Answer: D ($y = -5x + 2$)

Perpendicular slope = negative reciprocal of $\frac15$ = $-5$.

> **Trap:** (B) negates without flipping; (C) flips without negating. Both partial moves are always offered. Perpendicular = **flip and negate**, both steps.

---

## Q7 — Answer: 5

No solution ⇒ the lines are parallel ⇒ coefficient ratios equal, constant ratio different.

$$\frac{3}{9} = \frac{k}{15} \;\Rightarrow\; \frac13 = \frac{k}{15} \;\Rightarrow\; k = 5$$

Confirm it's parallel and not identical: $\dfrac{12}{18} = \dfrac23 \neq \dfrac13$ ✓ — different constant ratio, so no solution (not infinitely many).

> **Trap:** trying to solve for $x$ and $y$. There is no solution to find — that's the premise. Recognize the phrasing and go straight to ratios.

---

## Q8 — Answer: B (the flat service fee)

In $C = 45h + 90$, the 45 is multiplied by $h$, so 45 is the **hourly rate**; 90 stands alone, so it is the value of $C$ when $h = 0$ — the fee charged regardless of job length.

> **Trap:** (A) is the slope/intercept swap — the most common error in this question type. (C) is false on the arithmetic too: a 2-hour job costs $45(2) + 90 = \$180$, not \$90. The number *not* attached to a variable is always the starting/fixed amount.

---

## Q9 — Answer: 9

$$26 = 62 - 4t \;\Rightarrow\; 4t = 36 \;\Rightarrow\; t = 9$$

Check: $62 - 4(9) = 62 - 36 = 26$ ✓

> **Trap:** computing $26/4$ or $62/4$ instead of isolating first. Also note the candle burns out at $t = 15.5$ hours (where $h = 0$) — a common follow-up question on the same stem.

---

## Q10 — Answer: C (12)

Let $n$ = notebooks, $p$ = pens.

Counting equation: $n + p = 20$
Value equation: $3n + 1.75p = 50$

Substitute $p = 20 - n$:
$$3n + 1.75(20 - n) = 50$$
$$3n + 35 - 1.75n = 50$$
$$1.25n = 15 \;\Rightarrow\; n = 12$$

Check: 12 notebooks = \$36, 8 pens = \$14, total \$50 ✓

> **Trap:** (A) 8 is the number of **pens** — right work, wrong quantity. Label your variables with words ("$n$ = notebooks"), not just letters.
